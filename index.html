Discover Verde

A destination for coffee, sweets & something unexpected

Located in the heart of downtown Windsor, Verde Café is a destination for coffee, specialty drinks and indulgent desserts.

From classic espresso drinks and signature iced lattes to handcrafted mocktails, crêpes, waffles, mini pancakes and decadent cheesecakes, Verde Café offers something for every craving.

Whether you're stopping in for your morning coffee, meeting friends for something sweet, or treating yourself to one of Verde's signature creations, there's always something worth discovering.
